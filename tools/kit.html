3 · Tap-to-try NFC tag

Stick an NFC tag (NTAG213/215, a few cents each) on a poster, table tent or wristband. Phones open the AR page when tapped, no app needed.

Writing from this page needs Chrome on Android over https. On iPhone use a free app like “NFC Tools” → Write → URL.
