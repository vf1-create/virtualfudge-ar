### 2-minute phone checklist

Do this once on an iPhone and once on an Android phone. Turn the volume up.

1. Scan `print/qr-sign-808-4x6.png` on your screen with the phone camera. The TR-808 card should open, with the 808 rising into view. **Pass:** you hear nothing yet.
2. Tap any colored step pad. **Pass:** you hear a kick drum. Tap **▶ Play**: the pattern loops and a white outline moves across the pads. Drag the tempo slider: the speed changes. Try the **Late Night** and **Roller Rink** presets, then **■ Stop**.
3. iPhone only: flip the ring/silent switch to silent and tap a pad again. On iOS 17 and later you should still hear it. If you don't, note your iOS version.
4. Tap **See it in AR**. iPhone: Quick Look opens; point at the floor and the 808 lands at real size (about 68 cm wide). Android: Scene Viewer opens and does the same. **Pass:** you can walk around it.
5. Close AR. Tap **Spotify** under "Sexual Healing". **Pass:** your Spotify app (or the web player) opens a search for that song. No sound plays inside the demo.
6. Tap **← Map**, then tap **1983 TR-909**. **Pass:** its card and 3D model open.
7. Open the link with `?store=london` added. **Pass:** the header says Roland Store London, 10 Denmark Street, with 5 stations on a blue line.
