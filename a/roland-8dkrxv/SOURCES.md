# Sources

Every year and every song in the demo is listed here with its source. Anything we couldn't confirm was taken out (see "Removed" at the bottom).

**How we checked (Nov 3, 2026):** the build environment can't open Wikipedia, roland.com or the magazines directly. So each item was confirmed through search-engine extracts of the pages below, and the weakest few were searched again on their own. Click each link once before quoting any wording to a client.

## Removed (couldn't confirm, or sources disagree)
- Hungry Like the Wolf (Duran Duran): sources disagree on whether the lead was played on a Jupiter-8 or a Prophet-5.
- Rio (Duran Duran): Wikipedia says that was a Jupiter-4, not a Jupiter-8.
- Save a Prayer (Duran Duran): conflicting sources (a Roland article says it started on an SH-2).
- Can You Feel It (Mr. Fingers): a Juno-60 track, not a Juno-106.
- Revolution 909 (Daft Punk): the 909 use is confirmed, but album 1997 vs single 1998 is ambiguous. Easy to add back as "1997".
- Your Love, Confusion, Big Fun, Energy Flash, Thriller, Dirty Diana, Englishman in New York and various SH-101 / Juno-106 "artist used it" claims: no song-specific source.
